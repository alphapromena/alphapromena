## 2. What the Service Does

AlphaBeacon helps organizations maintain their LinkedIn company page presence. Each day, the Service drafts a set of candidate posts with accompanying images, grounded in the organization's own materials and publicly available market signals. A human reviewer selects, edits, and approves content before anything is published. Publishing to LinkedIn is performed only with the explicit authorization of an administrator of the relevant LinkedIn Page, through LinkedIn's official APIs.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Account information
When you create an account, we collect your name, business email address, organization name, and role. Authentication is handled through our identity provider; we do not store your password in plaintext.

### 3.2 LinkedIn authorization data
When you connect a LinkedIn company page, we collect and store:

- OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens issued by LinkedIn, used solely to publish content and manage posts on the Page you have authorized.
- The identifier and name of the LinkedIn organization Page(s) you connect.
- Metadata about posts published through the Service (post identifiers, publication timestamps, and status).

We request only the minimum LinkedIn permissions required to operate the Service. We do not collect, store, or process LinkedIn member profile data beyond what is strictly required to authenticate you and publish to the Page you administer.

### 3.3 Content and knowledge base data
To ground content generation in your organization's voice, we collect materials you choose to provide, including your company profile, brand voice guidelines, product sheets, case studies, past posts, and other documents you upload. We also store the drafts, images, rationales, and source citations generated by the Service, and the posts you approve and publish.

### 3.4 Feedback data
When you rate, comment on, edit, or reject a draft, we store that feedback and use it to improve the quality of future content generated for your organization.

### 3.5 Usage and technical data
We collect standard technical information such as log data, IP addresses, browser type, device information, and usage events within the Service. This data is used for security, debugging, and service improvement.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- Operate, maintain, and provide the features of the Service.
- Generate draft content grounded in your organization's materials.
- Publish content to LinkedIn Pages you have explicitly authorized, at your direction.
- Improve content quality for your organization through your feedback.
- Secure the Service, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.
- Communicate with you about the Service, including service updates and support.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your data to train models made available to other customers. Each organization's content, knowledge base, and feedback are logically segregated from those of other organizations.

## 5. How We Use LinkedIn Data

Our use of LinkedIn APIs and any data received from LinkedIn is governed by the LinkedIn API Terms of Use and the LinkedIn Marketing API Program terms. Specifically:

- We access LinkedIn only with your explicit OAuth consent, and only within the scopes you grant.
- LinkedIn access tokens are encrypted at rest and stored in a dedicated secrets management system with restricted access.
- We use LinkedIn APIs solely to publish and manage content on Pages for which you are an authorized administrator.
- We do not scrape LinkedIn, and we do not collect data about LinkedIn members or third-party organizations through LinkedIn's platform.
- If you disconnect your LinkedIn account or revoke authorization, we delete the associated tokens promptly.

## 6. Where and How Data Is Stored

The Service is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Credentials and API tokens are stored in AWS Secrets Manager. Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis and is logged.

## 8. Data Retention

- **Account data** is retained for as long as your account is active.
- **LinkedIn OAuth tokens** are retained only while a connection is active, and are deleted promptly upon disconnection, revocation, or account closure.
- **Content, knowledge base, and feedback data** are retained while your account is active or until you delete them within the Service.
- **Log and technical data** are retained for a limited period for security and operational purposes, then deleted or anonymized.

Upon account closure, we delete or anonymize personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
